Fuelvo privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Fuelvo is an app to compare fuel prices at nearby stations. It has no accounts and no ads, and it never sells or shares your data for advertising. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Germán de Bustamante Conde (“we”), the developer of Fuelvo, is the data controller. Contact: fuelvo.support@gmail.com.

Your preferences, favourite stations and recently loaded prices are stored only on your device, along with a copy of your price alerts. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If your phone's own backup is on (Google's or iCloud), it may include them, as it does other apps' data; on Android the backup never includes the identifier our server gave your phone for price alerts, so a restored phone starts without alerts.

Who else processes it

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.): usage statistics, only with your consent, crash reports (Crashlytics), and delivering notifications (Cloud Messaging), which receives your phone's push token and the notification. On iPhone it delivers them through Apple's push service (Apple Inc.).
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud): usage statistics, only with your consent. It also tells the app whether it is under maintenance, which uses a random identifier and no personal data.
  • Render and Neon: hosting of our server and its database, in the EU.
  • Google Maps (Android) and Apple Maps (iOS) draw the map; they receive the area shown, as any map does.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. They do so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

Usage statistics: at most 14 months. Crash reports: 90 days. Server logs: a few days. Price alerts and your phone's notification address: until you remove them, until 270 days after the app last contacted our server, or until Google tells us the app was uninstalled, whichever comes first. Data on your device: until you delete the app or its data.

Your choices and rights

  • You decide on usage statistics when you first open the app, and can change it at any time in Profile → Privacy → Share usage statistics. Turning it off stops the sending at once and erases the random installation id from your phone: if you turn it on again later, it starts with a new one, not linked to the earlier statistics.
  • Location is optional: the app works by choosing a province instead.
  • Notifications are optional. A price alert is removed from its station's bell, or by removing the station from your favourites. Profile → Notifications → Turn off all price alerts deletes every alert and your phone's notification address from our server. General notifications are off until you turn them on there, and you can turn them off the same way.
  • You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its use, or ask for a copy, by writing to fuelvo.support@gmail.com. As we don't know who you are, include the installation id if you have it, or tell us what you need and we will help.
  • You can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, or the one where you live.

Children

Fuelvo is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
